# POL-CAN-V1.2: Order Cancellation & Modification Policy
Version: 1.2
Effective Date: 2026-01-10

## 1. Cancellation Eligibility
- **Placed & Processing Status**: Customers may cancel any order free of charge at any time while the order is in `placed` or `processing` status.
- A 100% full refund will be immediately initiated to the original payment method.
- **Shipped or Delivered Status**: Once an order has reached `shipped`, `in_transit`, or `out_for_delivery` status, it cannot be cancelled or intercepted. Customers must receive the package and initiate a return request following our Return Policy.

## 2. Automated Self-Service Cancellation
- Orders can be cancelled autonomously through our customer support bot or self-service order portal.
- The system immediately verifies order status and triggers automated gateway refund release.

## 3. Order Address and Item Modifications
- **Shipping Address Modification**: Permitted only while the order is in `placed` status. Once processing begins in the warehouse, addresses cannot be updated.
- **Adding or Removing Items**: Individual items cannot be removed from an existing order. Customers are advised to cancel the order and place a new order with the desired configuration.

## 4. Cancellation by Seller
We reserve the right to cancel any order under the following circumstances:
- Sudden stockout or inventory discrepancy.
- Incomplete or fraudulent payment verification.
- Inability to deliver to the provided shipping destination by our courier partners.
In all seller-initiated cancellations, the customer receives an immediate notification and full refund.
