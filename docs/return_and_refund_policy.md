# POL-RET-V2.1: Return and Refund Policy
Version: 2.1
Effective Date: 2026-01-15

## 1. Overview
At our store, customer satisfaction is our top priority. We offer a comprehensive 30-day return policy for eligible products from the date of confirmed delivery.

## 2. Return Window
- Standard items can be returned within 30 calendar days from the date the shipment was delivered.
- Requests initiated after 30 days are strictly ineligible for return, replacement, or refund.
- The delivery date is determined by carrier tracking delivery timestamps.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
To qualify for a full return and refund:
- Items must be in their original, unused, and unworn condition.
- All original manufacturer tags, labels, warranty cards, and user manuals must be attached and intact.
- Items must be returned in their original packaging, including protective foam, dust bags, and accessories.
- A valid order number or proof of purchase is required.

## 4. Final Sale & Non-Returnable Items
The following items are designated as **Final Sale** and cannot be returned, exchanged, or refunded under any circumstances:
- Any product explicitly tagged as "Final Sale", "Clearance", or "As-Is" on the product detail page or invoice.
- Intimate apparel, swimwear, and personal hygiene products once opened.
- Custom-made, personalized, or engraved merchandise.
- Perishable goods, food items, and nutritional supplements.
- Downloadable software products, digital licenses, and gift cards.
*Exception*: If a Final Sale item arrives physically damaged, defective, or incorrect due to our warehouse error, report it within 48 hours of delivery for a replacement or store credit.

## 5. Refund Processing Timelines
- Once returned items are received at our fulfillment center, our inspection team inspects them within 2 to 3 business days.
- Upon inspection approval:
  - **Credit Card / Debit Card**: Refunds are initiated within 2 business days and reflect on your statement within 5 to 7 banking days.
  - **UPI / Net Banking**: Refund credited to original bank account within 24 to 48 hours.
  - **Store Credit / Wallet**: Issued instantly and never expires.
- Initial expedited shipping fees are non-refundable.

## 6. Return Shipping Fees
- **Defective, Damaged, or Incorrect Items**: 100% free return shipping. A prepaid shipping label will be generated.
- **Change of Mind / Customer Remorse**: A flat return processing fee of INR 100 will be deducted from the final refund amount.
